## 1. Franchise basics

- Original work: light novels by **Sunao Yoshida**, illustrated by **Thores Shibamoto**. Adapted into a manga (art by **Kiyo Kujō**) and a **24-episode anime** by **Gonzo** (2005). Continuity differs between the three versions, and the anime reorders and changes novel events.
- Genre: gothic sci-fi and fantasy, vampires, political intrigue, religious imagery, action.
- Anime opening theme, for reference only: "Dress (Bloody Trinity Mix)" by Buck-Tick. Do not use it.

## 2. World and setting

- **Time:** a far-future Earth, several hundred to about nine hundred years after **Armageddon**, a catastrophic war between humans and the vampire-like Methuselah that nearly destroyed civilization and the planet.
- **Methuselah:** the "Long-Lived Race". Backstory: humans who colonized Mars and became infected by a bacillus. They need blood to replenish what the bacillus consumes, have supernatural strength, can have children, and eat normal food. In the anime some can fly. They call humans **Terrans**.
- **The Vatican (Papal State):** the human government, based in Rome, ruled by a young and timid **Pope Alessandro XVIII**, advised by **Cardinal Caterina Sforza**. Home of a heavily militarized, politically divided church.
- **The New Human Empire (Methuselah Empire):** governed by an **Empress** in the anime. Capital drawn from Byzantium/Istanbul with Ottoman influence (including Janissary-style imperial guards). It is the most technologically advanced power, with weapons and machines beyond the Vatican and Albion.
- **Albion:** the British Isles human state, officially neutral between the Vatican and the Empire.
- **Cold war:** the Vatican and the Empire avoid open war. Peace is fragile. Factions on both sides want peace; extremists want war.
- **Visual and cultural mix:** Roman Catholic institutions and iconography, Byzantine and Ottoman imperial architecture, Renaissance Italian titles (e.g. Duchess of Milan), airships, advanced weapons and nanotechnology.

## 4. Core characters

Use these as fan-game cast. Design sprites from the owner's reference images, not from official art.

- **Abel Nightroad** (born Abel Knightlord): Vatican priest and AX agent. On the surface clumsy, absent-minded, tea-obsessed, with crooked glasses, tall and lanky in black clerical clothing. Truly a **Crusnik**, a powerful being created with nanomachines, who feeds on other vampires. Codename in the anime: **Crusnik 02**, which is also the activation code for his nanomachines. When he fights seriously his personality and appearance change and he becomes terrifying. He carries enormous guilt, is known as an atoner, and is afraid of Esther seeing his true form.
- **Esther Blanchett:** a young Terran nun, brave and gentle, with a tragic past. Meets Abel early, joins AX as his partner. Later revealed to have a significant royal heritage in the series.
- **Tres Iqus:** Vatican android priest and gunslinger, emotionless, highly efficient, loyal to Caterina Sforza, dual pistols, nickname "Gunslinger".
- **Caterina Sforza:** Cardinal, Duchess of Milan, strong-willed leader of AX and one of the Pope's chief advisers. Believes in peace between humans and Methuselah. Her older brother, a hawkish Cardinal, opposes her.
- **Pope Alessandro XVIII:** young, timid, kept at the center of Vatican politics.
- **Dietrich von Lohengrin:** Rosenkreuz Orden member, codename "Puppeteer".
- **Isaak Fernand von Kämpfer:** Rosenkreuz Orden member, codename "Panzermagier".
- **Astharoshe Asran:** Methuselah noble (Duchess of Kyiv, Viscountess of Odesa), powerful figure in the Empire, close to Abel's story.
- **Ion Fortuna:** Methuselah noble, the Count of Memphis, an envoy who works for peace.
- **Cain Nightlord/Nightroad:** Abel's twin brother (in the anime), a Crusnik and the principal antagonist; killed their sister Lilith. Hostile to Abel.
- **Seth Nightroad:** Crusnik, the youngest sibling, aligned with the Empire's founder faction *(role differs by version; verify)*.
- **Lilith Sahl:** the prototype Crusnik, "the Black Saint". Her death is the emotional root of Abel's guilt. She is buried at the Vatican.
- **Other AX members seen in the anime:** William Walter Wordsworth, Vaclav Havel, Noélle Bor.

*(unverified)* Specific villains and one-off arc antagonists (e.g. Gyula Ka'da'r, Archbishop D'Annunzio) belong to individual story arcs. Confirm which arcs the owner wants before building bosses around them.

## 5. Crusnik backstory (spoilers)

- Abel, Cain, Seth and Lilith were four "test tube" siblings created about a thousand years before the main story for the Mars Colonization Project and infused with Crusnik nanomachines. Lilith was the prototype, Seth the youngest, Cain the strongest.
- Abel, Cain and Seth sided with the Methuselah during the great war; Lilith sided with humanity and the Vatican that guarded it.
- Cain killed Lilith to silence her. In grief Abel carried her body to the Vatican, where she was buried. He stopped fighting and spent roughly nine hundred years mourning in her crypt, until a young Caterina Sforza fled there from assassins, and he joined AX.
- The nanomachines in Lilith's crypt become a plot item late in the story.

## 6. Themes to preserve in the game

1. **Coexistence versus extremism:** peace is fragile and costly, and both sides contain people who want it and people who don't.
2. **Duality:** gentle clumsy priest versus the Crusnik. Gameplay idea: a risk/reward transformation or an unstable power meter.
3. **Atonement and guilt:** Abel's past shapes his motives and the story's tone.
4. **Faith versus science:** holy relics and ritual beside nanotechnology and machines.
5. **Prejudice:** Terrans and Methuselah distrust each other; witches and outsiders are hated by both.
6. **Brotherhood and betrayal:** the Cain and Abel parallel.
7. **Sacrifice:** characters give up safety, memory or life for peace.

## 7. Tone and visual language

- **Palette direction:** deep black and charcoal clerical clothing; Vatican gold and white marble; blood crimson; cold steel and pale blue for technology and Crusnik energy; sickly green for nanomachine and lab motifs; candle-amber lighting.
- **Architecture:** Gothic cathedrals, stained glass, catacombs, Byzantine domes and minarets, imperial palaces, ruined cities, airship docks, gothic laboratories, crypts with ancient machinery.
- **Costume language:** long coats, clerical cassocks, high collars, crosses and rosaries as weapon motifs, military-church uniforms, imperial regalia.
- **Mood:** melancholic, tense, beautiful decay, solemn with bursts of violent action and occasional awkward comedy from Abel.
