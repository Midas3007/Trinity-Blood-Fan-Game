## Premise (1–3 sentences)

**"Requiem for Rome"** — an original side story set in Rome during the anime's run. A Rosenkreuz Orden operative, Dietrich von Lohengrin ("the Puppeteer"), turns the catacombs beneath the Vatican and a ruined quarter of the old city into a trap, filling them with puppeted thralls. His goal is to force Abel to reveal the Crusnik in front of witnesses and turn the Vatican against its own agent, breaking AX and pushing Rome toward war with the Empire.

**Why this is the easiest to build:** one city, two connected regions plus a hub; it doesn't retell a specific episode (so no scripts or scenes to match), and the "puppeteer" villain explains every enemy type (string-controlled thralls, marionette soldiers, puppeted Methuselah) with one idea.

## Where in the series does it take place?

During the anime, as an **original side story between arcs** in Rome. Abel already works for AX with Esther as his partner; Tres and Caterina are active. The deep Crusnik-sibling history is only hinted at (no Cain reveal) so the game doesn't contradict the anime's later episodes.

## Main characters in the game

- **Playable:** Abel Nightroad.
- **Allies:**
  - **Cardinal Caterina Sforza** — gives the mission at the AX hub; voice of the peace faction.
  - **Sister Esther Blanchett** — goes missing in the catacombs early; rescuing her is Abel's personal stake. She is the witness Abel most fears seeing his true form.
  - **Tres Iqus** — appears at fixed points to hold a door or give covering fire in one set piece; never playable.
- **Villains:**
  - **Dietrich von Lohengrin, "the Puppeteer"** (Rosenkreuz Orden) — main antagonist, manipulator behind every encounter.
  - **The Marchesa of the Catacombs** *(original character)* — a Methuselah noble Dietrich has puppeted against her will; tragic first boss.
  - **The Iron Choir** *(original)* — Dietrich's marionette soldiers in Vatican-style armour, used to make the attacks look like an inside job.

## Story beats (rough order)

1. **Opening / inciting event:** a night requiem mass in an old basilica above the catacombs. Worshippers vanish into the crypts, Esther among them. Caterina sends Abel down alone and quietly. *(Tutorial: move, attack, jump, dodge, heal.)*
2. **First region and boss — The Catacombs of Saint Sebastian** *(fictional version)*: ossuaries, flooded crypts, chapels. Abel finds the missing worshippers turned into string-bound thralls. Boss: **the Marchesa**, a Methuselah noble with strings in her veins. Defeating her frees her; she dies telling Abel who did it. Abel uses **Crusnik 40%** for the first time to survive — and Esther, half-conscious, *almost* sees it. *(Unlock: Crusnik 40% → **Crusnik leap** (double jump, no wings: wings only appear at 80%).)*
3. **Mid-story twist:** Vatican Inquisition-style troops arrive and start hunting "the vampire in the catacombs" — meaning Abel. The Iron Choir wears their colours. Abel must avoid hurting humans who think he is the monster.
4. **Second region and boss — The Drowned Quarter:** a flooded, ruined district of old Rome, aqueducts, a collapsed opera house where Dietrich stages his "performance". *(Unlock: Crusnik phase-step → dash through sealed iron grilles.)* Boss: **Dietrich's Grand Marionette**, a giant puppet built from church bells and armour, Dietrich controlling it from the rafters. Phase 2: cut the strings and Dietrich fights at range, then flees (he survives — consistent with the anime).
5. **Ending:** Abel carries Esther out at dawn. She says she saw "an angel with black wings" and doesn't know whether to be afraid. Caterina quietly buries the Inquisition report. Final scene: a coffin in the crypt where something — a hint of the Crusnik past — has been disturbed. Dark, unresolved.

## Things I definitely want

- **Abilities:** all of Abel's anime abilities that fit a 2D action game (see the mapping in `docs/creative-direction.md` §5): revolver, Crusnik transformation at 40% and a late, limited 80%, scythe, black wings (double jump/glide), phase-step dash, blood drain from vampires.
- **Bosses:** the Marchesa (region 1), Dietrich's Grand Marionette + Dietrich (region 2). Planned optional boss after the slice: a Rosenkreuz duellist *(original)*.
- **Locations:** AX hub inside the Vatican, catacombs, drowned quarter, ruined opera house.
- **Scenes:** first Crusnik activation; Esther almost seeing; Tres holding a door; dawn ending.

## Things I do not want

- No retelling of specific anime episodes, no anime dialogue, no official art.
- No full reveal of Cain or the Crusnik origins (spoiler control).
- No comic-relief scenes during the main story; Abel's clumsy humour only in short hub moments.

## Tone

**Dark.** Melancholic, tense, religious horror. Violence is sudden and costly. Abel's goofiness appears only as small character beats in the hub, as contrast.
