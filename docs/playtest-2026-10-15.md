# Automated playtest — live build, 2026-10-15

**How:** headless Chromium on the live Vercel site (`?test` read-only hook), phone-sized landscape screen (844×390, touch device), driven with **simulated multi-finger touch** on the on-screen stick and buttons. Game state recorded every frame.

**Limits:** this is not a real phone. It cannot judge feel, comfort, real frame rate, heat or audio. Frame rate below is from a desktop CPU.

## Bug found and fixed
- **Hit freeze was not visible.** Physics stepped once more before the freeze began, so knockback leaked through. Fix: physics pauses the moment a hit lands (`PlayScene.wireFeedback`). Re-measured after the fix: the game now freezes on every hit.

## Not covered (still needs the owner's phone)
Feel (heavy/floaty, slow/fast), button comfort and reach, notch/rounded-corner overlap, real-phone frame rate and heat, PWA install/offline on the device.
