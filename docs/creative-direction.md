# Creative Direction (draft for owner approval)

Do not generate final art until the owner approves this document. Story source: `docs/lore/owner-storyline.md` (proposal, delegated by the owner).

## 1. Working title
**Trinity Blood: Requiem for Rome** (unofficial fan game).

## 2. Premise
During the anime, Rosenkreuz operative Dietrich von Lohengrin ("the Puppeteer") turns the catacombs and a drowned quarter of Rome into a stage, using string-bound thralls and puppeted soldiers to force Abel Nightroad to reveal the Crusnik in front of witnesses, breaking AX and pushing Rome toward war. Abel must descend, save Esther, and stop the "performance" without becoming the monster everyone is hunting.

## 3. Setting
Rome, during the anime, at night. Three connected areas:
- **AX hub** (Vatican): a quiet office-chapel with Caterina, a save altar, an upgrade table. Warm candlelight; the only safe place.
- **Region 1 — Catacombs of Saint Sebastian** (fictional): ossuaries, bone-lined corridors, flooded crypts, small chapels, strings strung across tunnels.
- **Region 2 — The Drowned Quarter:** ruined streets half under water, aqueduct arches, scaffolding, a collapsed opera house.

## 4. Narrative themes
1. **Duality:** the gentle priest versus the Crusnik. Using Crusnik power is strong but costly (meter drain, and the story fear of being seen).
2. **Being watched / exposed:** Dietrich's whole plan is to make Abel perform. Witnesses, audiences, theatre imagery.
3. **Prejudice and the fragile peace:** humans hunt Abel; a Methuselah noble is a victim, not a villain.
4. **Atonement:** Abel frees the puppeted rather than just killing them (blood drain on Methuselah; mercy on human thralls).
5. **Faith versus machine:** relics and requiem masses beside nanomachines and clockwork puppets.

## 6. Enemy factions
All original designs:
- **String-bound thralls** (human): worshippers controlled by glowing strings; slow, grab attacks. Cutting the string above them stuns them. Mercy theme: they collapse, not die.
- **Puppeted Methuselah:** fast and aggressive; blood-drainable.
- **The Iron Choir:** marionette soldiers in Vatican-style plate armour with hymn-singing voice boxes; shields, halberds, ranged censers.
- **Catacomb vermin:** bats, bone-rats (small filler enemies).
- **Inquisition hunters** (human, region 2): you can't kill them — evade or knock out.

## 7. Boss concepts
1. **The Marchesa of the Catacombs** (Region 1): a Methuselah noble hung from strings like a chandelier. Phase 1: ground fight while she's jerked around by the strings (erratic lunges). Phase 2: Abel goes Crusnik 40% (scripted first activation); she fights back free of the strings, sorrowful and fast. Arena: a round ossuary chapel.
2. **Dietrich's Grand Marionette** (Region 2): a giant puppet made of church bells, organ pipes and armour, in the ruined opera house. Phase 1: dodge sweeping bell attacks, shoot the control strings. Phase 2: strings cut → Dietrich attacks from balconies with string traps. Ends with scripted Crusnik 80% and Dietrich's escape.
3. *(Post-slice, optional)* **A Rosenkreuz duellist** *(original)* — a parry-heavy one-on-one fight.

## 10. Art direction
- **Base resolution:** 480×270 (already in the game). One pixel scale everywhere; no rotating or non-integer scaling of sprites (CLAUDE.md Part 6).
- **Sprite size:** Abel ≈ **40 px tall** in a **64×64 frame** (room for coat tails and scythe arcs). Common enemies 32–48 px tall; bosses up to ~200 px.
- **Tiles:** 16×16.
- **Outline:** dark selective outline (`#0b0a10` outside, lighter inner lines); no pure-black interiors.
- **Lighting:** one cool moonlight key from above; warm candle pools as local light; Crusnik form adds a pale-blue rim light. Parallax: 3 layers max on phones.
- **Animation:** 8–12 fps hand-timed frames; holds on attack impacts. Code-driven effects (sparks, string glow, screen shake) where possible.
- **AI art (Higgsfield):** concept art, portraits, backgrounds only — into `art/concepts/`, never shipped directly.

## 11. UI direction
- **HUD (top-left, clear of the debug corner's tap area):** crimson health bar in a thin gothic frame; pale-blue Crusnik meter under it; holy water vials as small icons; revolver rounds as six dots.
- **Fonts:** a clean pixel serif for titles, a small readable pixel sans for text (licensed/OFL only).
- **Menus:** dark stone panels with gold trim, cross motifs used sparingly.
- **Dialogue:** bottom box with a portrait, short lines; Abel's humour only in the hub.
- **Map:** simple room grid with altar and gate icons.
- **Touch:** current layout kept; the ABIL button shows a hold-ring when Crusnik is available.

## 12. Audio direction
- **Music:** sparse pipe organ, low choir pads, distant bells; boss themes add percussion and strings. Original compositions or CC0/CC-BY only, credited in the asset manifest.
- **Ambience:** dripping water, wind through catacombs, creaking strings, faint singing from the Iron Choir.
- **SFX:** heavy, dry impacts; revolver crack; scythe whoosh with a metallic ring; a distinct "Crusnik activation" cue (deep pulse + rising tone).
- **Voice:** none; text only.
- No ripped anime or game audio (CLAUDE.md Part 6).
